07 · FAQ

Questions businesses usually ask.

Do we need an AI team to work with you?

No. The starting point is the business workflow. We can help with discovery, prototype development and the full technical implementation — your team provides business context.

Can you connect AI to our existing databases and tools?

Yes, where the required integration is technically and operationally appropriate. The design can include APIs, databases and controlled tool interfaces such as MCP.

Will the AI make decisions without approval?

That depends on the workflow. Approval steps, validation and exception handling can be designed into the process so sensitive actions are always reviewed by people.

Can we start with a small project?

Yes. A focused proof of concept is often the most practical starting point before expanding to additional workflows.

Do you only work with Bangalore companies?

No. Bangalore is our initial focus, but projects can be delivered for businesses elsewhere in India depending on the engagement scope.

How long does a typical project take?

A discovery engagement typically takes one to two weeks. A focused proof of concept can be completed in four to eight weeks depending on complexity and integration requirements.
